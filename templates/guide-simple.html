Finish your simple setup

Follow the numbered steps below. After you add your domain to Cloudflare (steps 1 and 2), the installer can do the rest of the Cloudflare setup for you with an API token (step 3). You can also do it by hand: add the DNS records, install the certificate and enable Full (strict) (steps 4 to 6). Then open your hotel and change the initial admin password.

This guide focuses on getting your hotel online. Selecting this guide does not change SSH, RDP, firewall, updates or backups.
