Open your hotel

  1. Open {{SITE_URL}} in your browser.
  2. Log in with the username {{ADMIN_USER}} and the password the installer showed you. The password is also saved in {{CREDENTIALS_FILE}}. Show it with {{CREDENTIALS_COMMAND}}.
  3. Change your password under Settings → Password.
  4. Click Enter hotel. The game client opens and connects.

Update

Get the newest emulator, client and hotel files with {{UPDATE_RUN}}. It makes a backup first and keeps your settings.

Backups

Every night at 04:00, when this PC is on, the installer backs up the database and your settings to {{BACKUP_DIR}}. It keeps the newest 7 backups. Make a backup now with {{BACKUP_RUN}}.

The backups are on this PC. Copy them to another place if your hotel matters to you.

Connect to your database

Connect to MariaDB at 127.0.0.1:3306, database plus, username hotel. The password is in {{CREDENTIALS_FILE}}: DB_PASSWORD on Linux, DbPassword on Windows.

What this local hotel does not do

This hotel is for this PC only. Other computers and phones cannot open it. It has no HTTPS, no Cloudflare and no protection for a public hotel. Do not use it as your public hotel.

The installer did not change your firewall, SSH, system updates or network settings.

Move to a real server later

  1. On your server, run the installer without --local: 
    {{SERVER_INSTALL}}
    It asks for your domain and sets up HTTPS, Cloudflare and the server protection. This makes a new, empty hotel.
  2. To keep your users, rooms and furniture, copy database.sql.gz from a backup in {{BACKUP_DIR}} to the server. On a Linux server, run in the folder with the file: 
    sudo systemctl stop plusemu
gunzip -c database.sql.gz | sudo mariadb plus
sudo systemctl start plusemu
  3. Run the installer on the server once more. It writes the server's own addresses into the website settings. Your accounts keep the passwords they had on this PC.
