Advanced setup checklist

Complete the same Cloudflare and certificate steps below, then review these manual administration tasks. Selecting this guide does not change SSH, RDP, firewall, updates or backups.

  • Administrator access: verify a named administrator and recovery access. On Linux, verify a separate SSH-key login and sudo before disabling root or password SSH. On Windows, verify your administrator's remote access and recovery method before restricting RDP or SSH.
  • Firewall: review public ports and administration access. If you restrict web ingress to Cloudflare, keep its address ranges current and verify both the website and game connection afterward.
  • Maintenance: choose update and reboot windows, review logs and disk space, and arrange local database and file backups with retention limits. Test a restore. Local backups cannot recover a lost server.

These tasks are not marked complete by the installer. Keep your existing administration connection open while changing access rules.
