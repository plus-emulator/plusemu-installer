1

Add your domain to Cloudflare

  1. Go to dash.cloudflare.com and create a free account (or log in).
  2. Click Add a domain (sometimes called Add a site), type {{DOMAIN}} and click Continue.
  3. Choose the Free plan at the bottom and click Continue.
2

Point your domain to Cloudflare

Cloudflare now shows you two nameservers that look like anna.ns.cloudflare.com. Put them in at the company where you bought your domain:

  1. Log in where you bought {{DOMAIN}} (Namecheap, GoDaddy, Porkbun, …).
  2. Find your domain's Nameservers setting. On Namecheap: Domain List → Manage → Nameservers → Custom DNS.
  3. Remove the old nameservers, paste the two from Cloudflare and save.
  4. Back in Cloudflare, click Check nameservers.
This usually takes 5–30 minutes (rarely up to 24 hours). Cloudflare emails you when your domain is active. You can carry on with the next steps in the meantime.
3

Add your DNS records

In Cloudflare, open your domain and go to DNS → Records.

  1. Delete any existing records named {{DOMAIN}} (or @) and www of type A, AAAA or CNAME. Cloudflare sometimes copies old ones over.
  2. Click Add record and add the records below. Leave the Proxy status switch orange (Proxied).
{{WS_DNS_ROW}}
TypeNameIPv4 addressProxy status
A@{{SERVER_IP}}Proxied (orange cloud)
Awww{{SERVER_IP}}Proxied (orange cloud)
The cloud must be orange. A grey cloud shows your server's IP address to everyone and disables the protection.
4

Turn on HTTPS the right way

  1. Go to SSL/TLS → Overview, click Configure and choose Full. Save.
  2. Go to SSL/TLS → Edge Certificates and switch on Always Use HTTPS.
  3. Go to Network and check that WebSockets is On. It's on by default, and the game needs it.
Why “Full”? The installer gave your server its own certificate, so the connection is encrypted all the way. Flexible causes “too many redirects”, and Full (strict) shows error 526.

Something isn't working?

Cloudflare error 521 or 522 Cloudflare can't reach your server. Check that the server is running and that the DNS records point to {{SERVER_IP}}. Some hosting companies also have a firewall in their control panel: allow ports 80 and 443 there.
Cloudflare error 526 Set SSL/TLS → Overview to Full, not Full (strict).
“Too many redirects” SSL/TLS is set to Flexible. Change it to Full.
The client loads but disconnects, or stays on the loading screen Check that WebSockets is on in Cloudflare (Network), then restart the emulator: {{RESTART_COMMAND}}. Still stuck? Watch the log while you open the client: {{LOG_COMMAND}}.
Asking for help on DevBest Post what you did, what you see (a screenshot helps) and the last lines of the emulator log. Never post your passwords file.
